Websites

Look as good as your work

Most people now visit charity websites on a phone. A slow or awkward site, and especially a clumsy donation page, quietly turns supporters away.

  • Homepage refreshI build a cleaner, faster homepage on the platform you already use, such as WordPress, Wix or Squarespace.
  • A donation page that worksI set up suggested amounts, one-off and monthly donation options in an easy to use system for your supporters.

Reports and data

Show what you achieve

Funders, trustees and supporters want to see the difference your work makes. I turn your records into clear reports and charts, so your results speak for themselves.

  • Annual and impact reportsI gather your figures and stories, write the text and lay out the pages, ready to print or share as a PDF.
  • Making sense of your dataI bring your survey records, notebooks or old spreadsheets into one tidy dataset, with charts and a short summary of what has changed.

What the sample chart tells us
